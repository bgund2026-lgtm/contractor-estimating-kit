## 7. Warranty Terms

**Workmanship:** [Your Company Name] warrants all labor for a period of [2] years from the date of completion. This covers defects in installation — not normal wear, abuse, or maintenance neglect.

**Materials:** Material warranties are provided by the manufacturer and vary by product. We pass through all manufacturer warranties to the client.

**What's NOT covered:**
- Damage caused by client, other contractors, or acts of God
- Normal wear and maintenance items (caulk joints, grout sealing, paint touch-up)
- Client-supplied materials (warranted by their manufacturer only)

## 8. Change Order Policy

Any work not described in this proposal requires a written Change Order signed by the client before work proceeds. This includes:

- Additional work requested by the client
- Unforeseen conditions discovered during demolition (e.g., water damage, knob-and-tube wiring, structural issues)
- Changes to materials, layout, or scope after work has begun

**Change order pricing:** 1.5× standard rate for labor, materials at cost + 20%. This reflects the disruption of mid-project changes and the cost of context-switching.

**No work proceeds without a signed change order.** This protects both parties.
